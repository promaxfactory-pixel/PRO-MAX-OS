## ASCII Relationship Summary

```
COMPANY SETTINGS (1) ──────► all financial defaults, factory profile

USERS ◄──────────────────────► ROLES ◄──────────────────────► PERMISSIONS
    │                              │                              │
    ├── audit_logs                 ├── user_roles                 └── role_permissions
    ├── login_attempts
    ├── password_change_attempts
    └── created_by (journals, docs, etc.)

CHART OF ACCOUNTS
    accounts (code PK)
        ├── journal_entry_lines (FK)
        ├── expenses (FK)
        ├── petty_cash_transactions (FK)
        └── inventory_items (implied via GL)

PRODUCTS (cups/cartons) ◄──────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │                                                              │
    ├── product_prices (per-customer)                              │
    ├── product_price_history (audit)                              │
    ├── product_families (grouping)                                │
    ├── bom (consumes inventory_items)                             │
    ├── inventory_items (linked finished goods)                    │
    ├── sales_invoice_lines (sold)                                 │
    ├── production_lines (produced)                                │
    ├── operations_daily_sheets (produced)                         │
    ├── production_shift_lines (produced)                          │
    ├── worker_daily_production (breakdown)                        │
    ├── local_supplier_exchanges (barter)                          │
    └── e_invoices (ZATCA XML)                                     │
                                                                   │
INVENTORY ITEMS (raw/pack) ◄───────────────────────────────────────┘
    ├── inventory_movements (all stock changes)
    ├── purchase_lines (received)
    ├── bom (component)
    ├── stock_transfers (moved)
    ├── shift_inventory_snapshots (counted)
    ├── inventory_adjustments (corrected)
    └── import_shipment_allocations (landed cost)

CUSTOMERS ◄────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    ├── customer_aliases (matching)                                │
    ├── sales_invoices (billed)                                    │
    ├── customer_payments (receipts)                               │
    ├── credit_notes (returns)                                     │
    └── product_prices (specific)                                  │
                                                                  │
SALES INVOICES ◄───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
    ├── sales_invoice_lines (items)
    ├── customer_payments (allocated)
    ├── credit_notes (referenced)
    └── e_invoices (ZATCA generated)

SUPPLIERS ◄────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    ├── supplier_price_history                                     │
    ├── purchases (POs)                                            │
    ├── supplier_payments (paid)                                   │
    ├── import_shipments (China imports)                           │
    └── local_supplier_exchanges (barter)                          │
                                                                  │
PURCHASES ◄────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
    ├── purchase_lines (items)
    └── supplier_payments (allocated)

PRODUCTION ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────►
    ├── production_orders (planned)
    │   └── production_lines (detail)
    ├── operations_daily_sheets (daily floor)
    │   └── production_shift_lines (per-worker)
    ├── worker_daily_production (per-worker summary)
    ├── shift_inventory_snapshots (stock counts)
    └── machines (equipment)

HR & PAYROLL ◄─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    ├── employees (master)                                         │
    ├── payroll_runs (periods)                                     │
    ├── payroll_run_lines (per-emp)                                │
    ├── employee_advances (salary advances)                        │
    ├── overtime_records (OT tracking)                             │
    ├── employee_leave_types (master)                              │
    ├── employee_leave_requests (workflow)                         │
    └── operations_daily_sheets / production_shift_lines (worker)  │
                                                                  │
FINANCE ◄──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
    ├── cashbank_accounts (bank/cash)
    │   └── cashbank_transactions (flow)
    ├── cheques (register)
    ├── renewals (doc expiry)
    ├── installments (loans)
    │   └── installment_payments (schedule)
    ├── petty_cash_accounts (funds)
    │   └── petty_cash_transactions (log)
    ├── custody_accounts (same)
    └── expenses (records)

E-INVOICE (ZATCA)
    ├── e_invoices (generated XML)
    ├── einvoice_settings (config)
    └── einvoice_queue (processing)

GOVERNMENT (Oman Compliance)
    ├── gov_entities (MOL, MoCI, ROP, etc.)
    ├── gov_integrations (API config)
    ├── gov_report_templates (reports)
    └── gov_submissions (tracking)

IMPORT TRACKING (China)
    ├── import_shipments (logistics)
    ├── import_shipment_costs (breakdown)
    └── import_shipment_allocations (landed cost)

BARTER EXCHANGE
    └── local_supplier_exchanges (bags ↔ cartons)

AUDIT & COMPLIANCE
    ├── audit_logs (full trail)
    ├── document_status_history (status changes)
    ├── document_voids (void records)
    └── daily_closings (daily snapshots)

MULTI-WAREHOUSE
    ├── multi_warehouse (locations)
    └── stock_transfers (moves)

ATTACHMENTS
    └── attachments (files on any entity)
```
